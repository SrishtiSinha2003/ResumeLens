Job Title: Data Scientist - NLP

We are hiring a Data Scientist to build and deploy NLP and machine learning
models for our product team.

Responsibilities
- Build NLP pipelines for text classification and information extraction
- Train and evaluate models using TensorFlow / PyTorch and scikit-learn
- Use Word2Vec and Transformer based embeddings for semantic similarity
- Perform feature engineering with pandas and numpy
- Deploy models to AWS and monitor performance

Requirements
- Master's degree in Data Science, Computer Science or related field
- Strong knowledge of Machine Learning, Deep Learning and NLP
- Experience with spaCy, BERT, scikit-learn
- Familiarity with Git and reproducible experiment tracking
